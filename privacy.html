Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Rose Rock Press LLC ("Rose Rock Press," "we," "us") is an independent publisher based in the United States. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

This website

This website is a simple information page. It has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records technical details of each visit, such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request, to run and secure the service. GitHub's handling of that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use that information to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation.

Reader newsletters

We send email newsletters to readers who sign up for them. If you sign up for one, we collect your email address, and your first name if you give it. We use them to send you the newsletter you asked for, including news about books, free stories and promotions.

Our email service provider, Kit, stores subscriber information and sends the emails on our behalf. Kit also records whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, which helps us learn what readers want more of. If you download a free book, our book delivery provider processes your email address to deliver the file.

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can unsubscribe at any time, and we will stop emailing you.

Advertising

We advertise our books on platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Amazon. If you click one of our ads or sign-up links, those platforms and our providers may record that the click happened, under their own privacy policies.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the business, such as our email, book delivery and hosting providers, and when the law requires us to.

How long we keep information

We keep newsletter subscriber information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep emails you send us for as long as we need them for business or legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. To make a request, email hello@roserockpress.com. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

Our website and newsletters are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.
